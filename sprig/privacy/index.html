Privacy Policy

Sprig — Plant Journal & Spirit Companion Bundle ID: com.sprig.bydfuh Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Sprig ("Sprig", "we", "us") handles when you use the Sprig iOS app, why it is needed, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it. By using Sprig you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information we handle

1.1 Account information

Sprig uses Quick Login. When you tap Quick Login, the app creates a random username (for example "Clara42") and a random password for you. We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number or any social login.

1.2 Plant and journal content you create

  • Plant profiles: nickname, species label, location label (free text such as "Kitchen sill" — not GPS), watering rhythm, acquisition date and cover photo.
  • Care entries: date, care actions (watering, misting, feeding, pruning), height and leaf counts, health observation, written notes and milestones.
  • Media you add to entries: plant photos, an optional "Together Moment" photo of you with your plant, short video clips, voice memos and their text transcripts.
  • Spirit companions: the spirit name, personality, appearance (a built-in illustration or an AI-generated look, see Section 5) and accessories.
  • Keepsakes: diary pages, growth books and spirit cards that you render inside the app.

1.3 Credits and purchase records

Your credit balance, a history of credit changes (purchases, renders, spirit generations, welcome gift) and the App Store transaction identifiers needed to avoid granting the same purchase twice.

1.4 Preferences

Settings such as default keepsake format, default watering rhythm, voice dictation on/off, appearance and ambience.

1.5 Information we do not collect

Sprig does not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, calendar, health data, advertising identifier or browsing history. Sprig does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. How we use information

We use the information above only to provide Sprig's features:

  • to show your garden, plant details, journal timeline and spirit companions;
  • to calculate vitality, care streaks, bond levels, growth stages and accessory unlocks from your care records;
  • to schedule care reminders;
  • to render keepsakes from your entries;
  • to generate an AI spirit look when you request one (Section 5);
  • to deliver and record credits you purchase.

3. Device permissions

Sprig asks for each permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can change any permission later in iOS Settings.

Permission Why Sprig asks
Camera To photograph your plants, take Together Moment photos and record short care clips. If camera access is unavailable, you can still log care in Pure Text Mode or import photos.
Photo Library (read) To choose existing plant photos for your journal or spirit generation.
Photo Library (add only) To save keepsakes (diary pages, growth book plates, spirit cards) to your Photos when you tap Save.
Microphone To record voice memos and the audio of care clips.
Speech Recognition To turn your voice memos into journal notes. When your iPhone supports on-device recognition, transcription happens on your iPhone; otherwise Apple's speech recognition service may process the audio under Apple's privacy policy.
Notifications To remind you when a plant is due for care. Sprig asks for notification permission when you first reach the Garden. Reminders are scheduled by the app on your iPhone.

4. Facial data

4.1 What we collect

Sprig does not use Face ID, TrueDepth facial mapping, facial recognition, facial geometry or any biometric identifiers, and it does not create faceprints. Sprig does not analyze faces in any image. Some photos and video clips you choose to add — in particular the optional Together Moment photo of you with your plant — may show your face or other people's faces. These are ordinary photos that you take or select yourself.

4.2 How we use it

Photos that may show a face are used only to display them inside your own journal (for example as the banner of a Journal post), to include them in keepsakes you choose to render, and to share or save them when you explicitly tap Share or Save. They are not used for identification, profiling, advertising or training any model.

4.3 Sharing and where it is stored

Together Moment photos, care clips and other entry media are saved within Sprig's app storage on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to us and are not sent to the AI image service. The only image ever sent to a third party is the single plant photo you choose when you tap Generate for a spirit look (Section 5); if that photo happens to include a person, the person will be part of that request, so we recommend choosing a photo that shows only your plant. Images leave the app otherwise only when you share or export them yourself.

4.4 Retention

Photos that may show a face are kept until you delete the entry, delete the plant, use Delete Account in Settings, or delete the app. Deleted media files are removed from Sprig's app storage immediately.

5. Third-party AI image generation

Sprig can create a personalized illustration of your plant's spirit companion using Agnes AI (apihub.agnes-ai.com), a third-party image generation service. This happens only when you tap Generate in Spirit Setup or Spirit Studio.

Data sent for each generation:

  • one plant photo that you select (resized to at most 1024 pixels), used as the reference image; and
  • a text prompt built from your plant's species label, plant category, the spirit personality you chose and the plant's most recent logged height.

Data never sent: your username or password, Together Moment photos, other journal photos, video clips, voice memos, transcripts, written notes, credit balance or purchase history.

Agnes AI returns one generated illustration, which Sprig saves as your spirit's look. Agnes AI processes the request under its own terms and privacy practices. If you prefer not to use this service, you can pick one of Sprig's built-in spirit illustrations instead; no data is sent in that case. The first generation for each plant is free; later generations use 1 credit.

6. In-app purchases

Credits are sold as consumable in-app purchases through Apple's App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; Sprig never receives or stores your card number or billing details. Sprig receives the purchase transaction information from Apple to deliver your credits.

7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Information leaves Sprig only:

  • when you request an AI spirit generation (Section 5);
  • when you share or export content yourself through the iOS share sheet or Save to Photos;
  • to Apple when you make a purchase or use speech recognition as described above;
  • if required by law.

8. Data retention and deletion

Your content is kept until you delete it. You can delete individual entries or plants at any time. Settings → Delete Account permanently removes your Quick Login credentials, all plants, care entries, photos, Together Moment photos, video clips, voice memos, transcripts, spirit looks, keepsakes, drafts, credit balance, credit history and preferences. This cannot be undone, and unused credits cannot be restored after deletion. Logging out keeps your data so you can sign back in with Quick Login.

9. Security

Sprig relies on iOS app sandboxing and data protection to keep your content private to the app. Requests to the AI image service are sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Children's privacy

Sprig is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information. You can view and edit your content directly in the app and delete it with Delete Account. For any other request, contact us at the address below.
